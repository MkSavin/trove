`trove client add kimi` connects Kimi Code, `--local` at its user level for every project, `--hooks` with entries in its user-level config. A `.ignore` at the store root keeps the archive out of ripgrep-based searches. While a store with `sync` on has uncommitted changes, every MCP tool result carries a notice asking for `trove_check` and `trove_sync`. `trove hook` reads Kimi Code's input and no longer exits 2 when it crashes.
