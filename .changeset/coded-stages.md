Plan stages are also read from a run of headings or bold paragraph labels coded like `Э1` or `Stage 2`, done by a `[x]` or `✅` mark, a done value in a trailing parenthesis or a status line under it. Stage titles and `next` lose their Markdown and keep their first sentence. The default done words cover more English and Russian forms (`released`, `слито`, `в master`, `реализовано` and others). Documents in the archive or with a status the archive keeps have no stages and leave the progress view.
